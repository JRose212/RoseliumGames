    Welcome to the Powderpuff Pokéball League

    A softer, sillier sister league to Pokéball. Same football, same fun, but only cute Pokémon can play, and there's no draft: you pick your whole team yourself. Here's how it all works.

    How to Join

    1. Go to League Home.
    2. Create an account with your email and a password, and give your team a name. This league has its own accounts, separate from Pokéball and Big Woof.
    3. Once the admin opens roster picks, head to My Team and fill your 14 spots.

    Your Team

    Every team has 14 spots, 7 on offense and 7 on defense. There's no bench and no kicker.

    OffenseDefense
    Quarterback (QB)Safety (S)
    Running Back (RB)Linebacker (LB)
    Blocker 1, 2 and 3 (BLK1–3)Tackle 1, 2 and 3 (T1–3)
    Wide Receiver 1 and 2 (WR1–2)Cornerback 1 and 2 (CB1–2)
    • Pick any playable Pokémon for any spot. Duplicates are fine: the same Pokémon can fill every spot on your team, and you can have the same Pokémon as anyone else, including your opponent.
    • The admin decides which Pokémon are playable. The Pokédex marks each one as playable or not.
    • Roster picks open when the admin switches them on. After that, change your lineup whenever you like - unless the admin has locked rosters for game day.
    • Drag a Pokémon onto another spot on My Team to swap them, or tap one and then tap the spot to swap it with.

    Season & Schedule

    • The regular season is 6 weeks, and every game is played on Sunday.
    • The admin can generate the schedule once at least 4 teams have joined.
    • A touchdown is worth 7 points. There are no kickers, no extra points and no safeties.
    • Regular-season games can end in a tie.
    • The admin posts each result on the Schedule page with the final score and a game summary, and names a Player of the Week with a write-up.

    Standings & Playoffs

    • Teams are ranked by wins, then by point differential. A tie doesn't count as a win or a loss.
    • Every team makes the playoffs, seeded by the final standings. The playoffs are single elimination and start the week after the regular season ends.
    • If the number of teams isn't 4, 8 or 16, the top seeds get a first-round bye.
    • Playoff games can't end in a tie.

    Daily Training

    Train your team from the My Team page to help your results. Training doesn't guarantee a win, but it can tip the scales. You get one session per real calendar day, with six to pick from:

    • Team Hike
    • Touchdown Celebration Choreo
    • Spa Day
    • Team Yoga
    • Play Outside
    • Gameplanning
    • Losing a game banks a bonus session, letting you train twice (two different types) on a later day. Bonus sessions stack across losses and clear the moment you win.
    • A tie doesn't add or clear bonus sessions.
    • A streak badge tracks consecutive days trained, shown on My Team and on the Rosters page.
    • The Schedule page shows what each team has trained since its last game.

    Cheerleaders

    Just for fun, you can add up to 5 cheerleaders from the Train Your Team section of My Team. Any Pokémon at all can cheer, including Legendaries and Pokémon that aren't playable. They don't affect your team or your results.

    Questions along the way? Ask in the league chat - it's open on every page.
