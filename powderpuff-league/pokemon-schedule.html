Schedule is locked

A result has already been recorded, so the schedule can no longer be regenerated normally. Resetting the season deletes every matchup, Player of the Week pick and the season state, so a fresh schedule can be generated from scratch.

Reset Training

Clears every team's training streak and banked bonus sessions, and deletes any training log entries from before the date below - useful for wiping out pre-season/test training once the real season starts.

Swap Teams in a Week

Pick a week and two teams, and they trade places in that week's schedule: each takes the other's opponent and home/away side. If one of them has the bye that week, the other gets the bye instead. Other weeks don't change. Games that are already final can't be changed.
