How to play

The core mechanics every 1v1 match runs on.

1. Setup

Each player takes their chosen hero's board, leaflet, and tokens. Set your Health Dial to 50 (in a 1v1 game) and your Combat Points (CP) Dial to its starting value. Shuffle your deck and draw your starting hand. Roll a die — highest goes first as the Start Player. Some heroes have unique setup steps listed under "Hero Setup" on their leaflet.

2. Turn phases

  1. Upkeep — resolve status effects and passive triggers.
  2. Income — gain CP and draw a card (the Start Player skips this on turn 1).
  3. Main Phase 1 — play Hero Upgrade or Main Phase Action cards, or sell cards for CP.
  4. Offensive Roll Phase — roll to activate an ability.
  5. Targeting Roll Phase — roll to pick a target (multiplayer only).
  6. Defensive Roll Phase — the defender may activate their Defensive Ability.
  7. Main Phase 2 — identical options to Main Phase 1.
  8. Discard Phase — sell cards down to a 6-card hand limit.

3. Dice & abilities

On your Offensive Roll Phase you roll your five dice up to 3 times, re-rolling any you like each attempt, chasing the Activation Requirement of a single Offensive, Defensive, Passive, or Ultimate Ability. Only one ability can be activated per Roll Phase — rolling the requirement for more than one just means you choose which to use.

4. Combat & damage

There are 5 damage types — normal, undefendable, pure, collateral, and ultimate — each with its own rules for whether it can be defended against, avoided, or modified. Normal damage lets the defender activate their Defensive Ability; the rest either bypass or restrict it. All damage and prevention effects accumulate and are applied simultaneously at the end of the Roll Phase.

5. Cards & CP

Action cards come in three flavors by border color: red Instant Actions (playable anytime, even to interrupt), blue Main Phase Actions (your turn only), and orange Roll Phase Actions (any Roll Phase, any player's turn). Spend CP to play them or to upgrade abilities — your CP Dial caps at 15. Sell unwanted cards during Main Phase or Discard Phase to refill it.

6. Winning

Reduce your opponent's Health to 0 to win. If all remaining players are reduced to 0 at the same time, the game ends in a draw. Every hero also has an Ultimate Ability, activated only by rolling five 6's — once triggered, nothing can prevent, reduce, or respond to its effects.

Glossary

Key terms used across hero boards and rulings.

Ability
Printed on the Hero Board, minion card, or boss panel. See Offensive, Defensive, Passive, and Ultimate Abilities for the different types.
Action cards
Single-use cards played for a benefit. Color-coded by when they can be played: Instant (red), Main Phase (blue), or Roll Phase (orange).
Active Player
The player whose turn it currently is.
Attack
An Offensive Ability that deals at least 1 damage to an opponent (not counting collateral damage).
Attack Modifier
Any card or status effect that modifies an Attack. Can only be used on Attacks, and only played on an Attack you're activating on your own turn unless stated otherwise.
Chaos Dice
The 5 dice used by Minions and Bosses in Dice Throne Adventures.
Combat Points (CP)
Spent to play cards and activate Hero Board abilities. Capped at 15; increases during your Income Phase.
Companion
A token, dial, or custom component some heroes control. Not a status effect, so immune to status-effect interactions, but can still be interrupted.
Damage types
Normal, undefendable, pure, collateral, and ultimate — each with its own defendable / avoidable / modifiable attributes.
Defender
The player being targeted by an Attack.
Defensive Ability
Activated during your Defensive Roll Phase if an opponent's Attack dealt Normal Damage. Denoted by an ability name and dice count over a green shield.
Final Dice Result
The result of your five dice after all Roll Attempts and dice modifications are finished.
Health Dial
Tracks your life total. At 0 you're defeated; you can heal a maximum of 10 above your starting Health.
Hero Upgrade
A card that permanently upgrades a named space on your Hero Board. Can be played directly to level III, paying only the difference in cost if upgrading from level II.
Inflict
Take the status effect's corresponding token and place it on the receiving player's Hero Board.
Offensive Ability
Activated during your Offensive Roll Phase. Only one can be activated per Roll Phase.
Passive Ability
An always-on effect described on the Hero Board; how it triggers varies by hero.
Roll Attempt
A dice roll performed to Activate an Offensive or Defensive Ability. You get up to 3 per Offensive Roll Phase.
Status effect
A positive or negative token gained or inflicted through cards or abilities. Some are "spendable" and can be used any time unless noted otherwise.
Ultimate Ability
Requires rolling five 6's to activate. Once triggered, no card, status effect, or ability of any kind can respond to or reduce it.
Upkeep Phase
The first phase of your turn — resolve any status effects, abilities, or Companions that trigger here.

Frequently asked questions

Common rulings that come up during a match.

General rules

What does "Chosen Player" mean, versus "Chosen Opponent"?

"Chosen Player" means you pick the person — that includes yourself, a teammate, or an opponent. "Chosen Opponent" means any player who is not you or on your team.

Can I interrupt a Main Phase or Roll Phase card by spending a status effect or playing an Instant Action?

Yes — status effects are considered Instant Actions. But you cannot interrupt an Instant Action or a spendable status effect the same way: once one is spent, its effects can't be interrupted.

If a card says "remove one status effect," do I remove one token or all of that type?

Just one token — each token is treated as its own discrete instance of that status effect. "Remove all status effects" means all of them, good or bad.

What is a Stack Limit?

The maximum number of tokens of a status effect a player may have at any time — e.g. "Stack Limit 1" means no more than 1 of that status effect at once.

If an opponent's ability does no damage, do I still get a Defensive Roll Phase?

No — to activate your Defensive Ability you must be Attacked for at least 1 point of defendable damage.

Can I use an Attack Modifier on an ability that doesn't deal damage?

No — the ability must be an Attack that deals at least 1 damage for an Attack Modifier to apply.

Does collateral damage automatically hit every opponent?

No — collateral damage has special targeting rules specified on the card or ability, and doesn't count as an Attack.

What happens if I run out of cards in my deck?

Shuffle your discard pile to create a new deck.

Status effects & cards

Can I upgrade an ability directly from level I to level III?

Yes — you pay the full cost of the level III upgrade. If you're going from level II to level III, you only pay the difference in cost.

Can I reduce or avoid undefendable damage with cards and status effects?

Yes — "undefendable" only means you can't activate a Defensive Ability against it. It can still be reduced, prevented, or avoided.

Can I play Attack Modifiers after my opponent's defense roll?

Yes — they can be played either before or after the defense roll is made.

Can I activate multiple abilities if I roll the requirements for more than one?

No — you may only choose one ability to activate per Roll Phase.

When I sell a card, how much CP do I gain?

1 CP per card sold — not the card's play cost.

Ultimate Abilities

Can any status effect or card reduce or prevent an Ultimate's damage or effects?

No. Nothing can stop an Ultimate once it's activated — no Heal Bot, no Instant Action cards, no Back Strike or Retribution, no blocking of any kind.

So how do I stop an opponent's Ultimate?

Alter their dice during their Offensive Roll Phase, before the ability is activated — once it's triggered, it's unstoppable.

Can I use Attack Modifiers to increase my own Ultimate's damage?

Yes — an Ultimate's damage is modifiable, even though it can't be defended against or avoided.

Dice Throne Adventures

What is Dice Throne Adventures?

A genre-bending cooperative expansion for Dice Throne. Take 1 to 4 heroes into randomly generated worlds with beautiful environments, intense boss battles, Diablo-inspired loot, and non-destructive legacy elements.

Can a Hero heal above their starting Health in Adventures?

Yes — same as standard Dice Throne, a Hero can heal up to 10 above their starting Health.
