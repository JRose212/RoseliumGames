    Welcome to Fantasy Pokéball

    Fantasy Pokéball is a fantasy football league where you draft Pokémon instead of NFL players onto a 26-slot roster, then manage your team all season through a weekly schedule, standings, and playoffs. Here's how everything works and where it all lives.

    How to Join

    1. Go to League Home.
    2. Create an account with your email and a password, and give your team a name.
    3. That's it - you'll show up in the manager list immediately, and you can start building your Pre-Draft Rankings right away.

    Your Roster

    Every team has 26 slots: 11 offense, 11 defense, a kicker, and 3 bench spots. Any Pokémon can play any position. Drag a Pokémon onto another slot on My Team to swap them, or on a phone tap one and then tap the slot to swap it with. Only starters record stats in box scores - bench Pokémon don't.

    How the Draft Works

    A snake draft across all 26 roster positions - pick order reverses every round.

    Each pick has a countdown timer. If yours expires, any signed-in manager can trigger your autodraft - it fills your highest-ranked still-available Pokémon from your own Pre-Draft Rankings into your first open slot, or skips the pick if your list is empty. You can rank up to 50 Pokémon, and nobody else can see your list.

    Legendary and Mythical Pokémon can't be drafted, ranked, or signed as free agents - they show up grayed out and labeled "Legendary" everywhere a Pokémon can normally be picked.

    Season & Schedule

    The regular season is 13 weeks. Games are played Thursday at 8pm, Sunday at 1pm, 4pm, or 8pm, and Monday at 8pm. The admin posts each result on the Schedule page with the final score, game summaries, and box score. If a final score is corrected later, the new score is announced in the league chat, and bonus training sessions are adjusted if the winner changes.

    Standings & Playoffs

    • Teams are ranked by wins, then by point differential. A regular-season tie doesn't count as a win or a loss.
    • The top 8 teams make the playoffs, which are single elimination and start in Week 14.
    • Quarterfinals (Week 14): 1 vs 8, 2 vs 7, 3 vs 6, and 4 vs 5.
    • Semifinals (Week 15): the 1 vs 8 winner plays the 4 vs 5 winner, and the 2 vs 7 winner plays the 3 vs 6 winner.
    • PokéBowl (Week 16): the two semifinal winners play for the championship.
    • Playoff games can't end in a tie - they go to overtime until there's a winner.

    Free Agency

    Once the season starts, any Pokémon nobody has drafted is fair game - sign one from the Pokédex page:

    1. Click an eligible Pokémon (not drafted, not Legendary) - a "Sign Free Agent" button appears.
    2. Choose a slot from your own roster to sign it into. If that slot is already filled, the current Pokémon there is dropped back into the pool as part of the same move.
    3. Confirm - the league chat announces it automatically ("Your Team dropped X for Y").
    • You can make 2 signings per week during the regular season. The limit resets once every game of the current week is final.
    • You can also drop a Pokémon without signing a replacement, using the "✕" next to it on the Rosters page. It returns to the pool, and that slot stays empty until you fill it.

    Trades

    Trades open once the draft is complete:

    1. On the Rosters page, click a Pokémon on another team.
    2. Choose what you'll receive and what you'll give, then send the proposal. Uneven trades are fine as long as both teams have room.
    3. The other manager accepts or denies it from My Team, and you can cancel your own proposal until they answer.
    • Pokémon in a pending trade can't be included in another trade until it's resolved.
    • During the regular season, a trade is blocked if one team has already played that week and the other hasn't, so no Pokémon can play twice in a week.
    • If either roster changes in a way that affects the trade, it can't be accepted and has to be proposed again.
    • Traded Pokémon move into the slots the other team gave up, then any open slots. Rearrange them on My Team afterward.
    • Proposals and completed trades are announced in the league chat.

    Cheerleaders

    Just for fun, you can add up to 5 undrafted Pokémon as cheerleaders from the Train Your Team section of My Team. They don't affect your roster or results. If one gets drafted or signed by any team, it leaves your cheerleader slot.

    What's Next

    The live draft date is still to be announced - get your account made and your Pre-Draft Rankings in so you're ready whenever it's set. Rankings are private, take a few minutes, and mean autodraft can still pick well for you if you ever miss your turn. After the draft, the admin will generate the regular-season schedule, and the season kicks off from there.

    Questions along the way? Ask in the league chat - it's open on every page.
