Roselium Games Season One

Every legend needs a proving ground.

This season, Roselium Games brings the full roster of Unmatched and Dice Throne champions to The Battle Standard for head-to-head duels where every hero fights like nobody else at the table.

Pick a fighter and you're not just choosing stats — you're choosing a playstyle. Every character comes with their own toolkit, their own win conditions, and their own way of turning a losing fight into a comeback. Rules take minutes to learn, but mastering a champion — reading your opponent, managing your resources, timing that one perfect play — can take a season.

Over five weeks, you'll battle for standings, build a match history, and find out which champion is really yours. Weeks one through four are open play — jump in, try new fighters, climb the leaderboard. Week five, the top contenders throw down in a single-elimination tournament to crown a season champion.

Quick to learn. Endless to master. One hero. One opponent. One winner.

Pick your champion. Enter the arena.

Dice Throne

Take the Throne

Dice Throne pits hero against hero in a duel of dice, cards, and combos — roll your fate, unleash devastating abilities, and grind your opponent's HP to dust. Fast, brutal, and endlessly replayable, it's the perfect blend of tabletop RPG flair and competitive board game strategy.

Unmatched

Become Unmatched

Unmatched throws legends from every corner of history and myth into the same arena, even some of your Marvel and movie favorites — anything goes. Deceptively simple card combat hides deep asymmetric strategy, where every hero plays like a completely different game.
